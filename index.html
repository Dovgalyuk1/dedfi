DEDFI

i take a cut of every meme coin trade on robinhood, and i spend it on stocks. the drawer does not open.

all day long people are out there on robinhood chain buying and selling meme coins, in and out, in and out, thousands of them an hour. every single one of those trades leaves a small fee on the counter, and i am the old man at the window who picks it up. nobody ever notices me doing it, which suits me fine.

that fee buys stock. real stock, the boring kind, the kind your uncle bought in 1986 and then forgot about for thirty years and accidentally got rich. it goes straight into the drawer and it stays there — not when it dips, not when the chat is screaming, not when a man in a good suit on television says that this time is different.

you hold the coin, your seniority accrues, and everything the drawer collects goes back out as dividends to the people who were still standing there. the longer you stayed, the bigger your share of it. that is the whole arrangement and i have not changed a line of it in forty years.

HOW THE MONEY GETS HERE

FORM 01-A

four steps. it has been four steps since before the computers, and nobody has managed to talk me into a fifth one.

1

THE TRADE

somebody somewhere on the chain buys a meme coin, as they do, constantly. a little piece of that trade gets peeled off at the window and written into the book by hand. it is not a large piece. it is just a very consistent one.

2

THE DRAWER

that piece buys stock and the stock goes into the drawer. the drawer has one direction, which is in. i lost the key to the other direction in 1994 and i have not looked very hard for it.

3

THE SENIORITY

while you hold, your seniority accrues. it accrues on weekends, it accrues on holidays, it accrues while you sleep. it stops the minute you walk out, and it starts over from zero when you come back.

4

THE DIVIDEND

what the drawer collects goes back out to whoever is still standing there, and the bigger your seniority the bigger your share of it. the people who left get a very polite letter and nothing else.
